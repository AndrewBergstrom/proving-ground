Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Who we are

Proving Ground is a personal, non-commercial study tool for practicing technical interview skills. This policy explains what data it handles and why.

What we collect

  • Account identity. If you sign in, we receive your email address and a user identifier from your chosen sign-in provider (email link, GitHub, Google, Apple, or Facebook) through our authentication provider, Supabase.
  • Your learning progress. Which modules you have completed and your spaced-repetition review schedule.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not use advertising or third-party tracking.

How your data is stored

  • In your browser. Progress is saved locally on your device so the app works without an account.
  • In our database. If you sign in, your completed modules and review schedule are stored in Supabase (a hosted Postgres database) so your progress syncs across your devices. Row-level security ensures you can only read and write your own data.

How your data is used

Only to save and sync your learning progress across your devices. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, and we do not use it for any purpose beyond running the app.

Third-party services

Sign-in and data storage are handled by Supabase. When you choose a social sign-in, that provider (GitHub, Google, Apple, or Facebook) processes your login under its own privacy policy. The app is hosted on Vercel.

Deleting your data

You can clear all local progress any time with the "Reset" button in the app. To delete your synced account data, email the address below and we will remove your record.
